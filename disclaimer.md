# Disclaimer

This repository documents the reverse engineering and static analysis
of malware samples for educational and defensive research purposes.

- No malicious code, functional exploits, or complete malware source is
  reproduced here — only descriptions of observed behavior patterns,
  annotated disassembly/decompiler excerpts used for teaching purposes,
  and analysis methodology.
- The goal is to build pattern-recognition skills for identifying
  malicious behavior in unknown binaries (a core skill in incident
  response, threat intelligence, and defensive security research).
- Conclusions in this repo reflect the author's current understanding
  and confidence level at time of writing, and may be revised as
  analysis continues. Where uncertain, this is noted explicitly.
